# MVP Rules

## Do Not Build These Yet

- No social features
- No community
- No gamification
- No complex dashboard
- No Apple Health sync
- No Screen Time API
- No wearable sync
- No long AI chat
- No therapy-style experience
- No diagnosis
- No medical claims
- No real-time high-frequency intervention
- No productivity police behavior

## Product Principle

The user should understand the output in under 10 seconds.

The daily check-in should take under 1 minute.

The product must always follow this structure:

State → Explanation → Decision

## Tone

The tone should be calm, direct, and useful.

Avoid:
- Therapist language
- Motivational quotes
- Corporate productivity language
- Over-scientific language
- Horoscope-style vague language

Good example:
"You are not lazy today. Your brain is likely overloaded from low recovery and high input."

Bad example:
"You should honor your emotions and take space for healing."

Bad example:
"Your cognitive retention index is reduced by attention residue accumulation."

## First MVP

Build only:

1. Morning check-in form
2. Rule-based status generator
3. Morning Report output
4. Night check-in form
5. Night Report output
6. Simple admin/testing log
